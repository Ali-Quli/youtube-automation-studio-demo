        Before you ship
        
The upload checklist

        
Pre-loaded with the rules that protect a faceless documentary channel. Every project in the full version carries this list into YouTube Studio, so a 1 a.m. upload never goes out half-finished.

      

      

        
1
“Made with AI” disclosure = Yes
AI-generated visuals and narration must be disclosed on every upload — no exceptions.

        
2
Chapters in the description
Timestamps from the script's chapter plan, starting at 00:00, pasted before publishing.

        
3
End screen added
Subscribe element + “best for viewer” video in the final 20 seconds.

        
4
Pinned comment posted
A question that invites discussion — posted and pinned right after the video goes live.

        
5
Added to the right playlist
Series playlist chosen in Studio so the next episode is one click away for the viewer.

        
6
Scheduled — never published live
Set the slot (e.g. Fri 3:00 pm ET / Sun 10:00 am ET) in advance instead of going live off-schedule.

        
7
Thumbnail passes the 1-second test
Max 3 elements, 2–4 words, readable at phone size, text names the type + the amount.

        
8
Title trigger check
The title fires Fear, Greed or Curiosity — and doesn't repeat the thumbnail's words.

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        Under the hood
        
How it's powered

        
The platform doesn't train its own AI. It rents the brain (a large language model API) and supplies the expertise: each expert is the same model with a different specialist instruction set, niche rulebook and output format. The rules are the product.

      

      

        
STEP 1You brief the expert
Topic, niche, length and language — thirty seconds of input.

        
STEP 2The rulebook writes the prompt
Your channel's rules — triggers, thumbnail limits, chapter format — are injected automatically.

        
STEP 3Work lands in the pipeline
Script, thumbnail, SEO pack and checklist attach to one project, ready to produce and upload.
